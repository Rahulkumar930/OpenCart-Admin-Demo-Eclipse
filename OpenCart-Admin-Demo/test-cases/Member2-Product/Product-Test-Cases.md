# Member 2 - Product Test Cases

## TC_PRODUCT_001 - Search Product

**Objective:** Verify product search.

**Steps:**
1. Open OpenCart.
2. Enter a product name in the search box.
3. Click Search.

**Expected Result:**
Matching products should be displayed.

---

## TC_PRODUCT_002 - Open Product Details

**Objective:** Verify product details page.

**Steps:**
1. Search for a product.
2. Click the product.

**Expected Result:**
Product details page should open.

---
